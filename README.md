# UK Driving Theory Test Trainer

A free, self-contained study tool for the UK car driving theory test. Covers all 14 official DVSA syllabus topics with revision notes, topic-by-topic practice questions, and a realistic mock exam.

No installation, no account, no server — it's a single HTML file that runs entirely in your browser.

## Features

- **Study notes** for all 14 DVSA topics (Alertness, Attitude, Safety and Your Vehicle, Safety Margins, Hazard Awareness, Vulnerable Road Users, Other Types of Vehicle, Vehicle Handling, Motorway Rules, Rules of the Road, Road and Traffic Signs, Documents, Incidents/Accidents/Emergencies, Vehicle Loading)
- **Topic practice** — untimed quizzes with instant feedback and explanations for every answer
- **Mock exam** — 50 random questions, a 57-minute countdown, a question navigator with flagging, and a pass mark of 43/50, matching the real test format
- **Progress tracking** — your scores and topic mastery are saved automatically in your browser (`localStorage`), so you can pick up where you left off next time
- Works on desktop and mobile browsers, light and dark mode

## Getting started

1. Download or clone this repo.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern browser (double-click it, or right-click → Open with → your browser).
3. Start with **Study Notes**, then **Practice** by topic, then try a full **Mock Exam** once you're comfortable.

No build step, no dependencies to install.

## Important note on content

The practice questions are original material written to match the DVSA theory test syllabus and the Highway Code. They are **not** the official DVSA question bank (which isn't publicly reproducible) — this is a revision aid, not a guarantee of the real exam's exact questions. Always refer to the official [Highway Code](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code) and the official [DVSA theory test revision materials](https://www.gov.uk/theory-test) as your primary source.

## Progress data

Progress is stored only in your browser's local storage on the device you use — it isn't sent anywhere, and isn't shared between devices or browsers. Clearing your browser's site data for this page will reset your progress.
